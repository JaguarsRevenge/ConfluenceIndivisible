Ballots mail ~Oct 16

Four steps to vote with confidence

  1. Verify your registration before ballots mail.
  2. Vote early, as soon as your ballot arrives.
  3. Use a drop box, not the mail.
  4. Track your ballot to confirm it was counted.

Covers Congress (Districts 4 and 8), Legislative District 12, and county races for commissioner, assessor, auditor, clerk, sheriff, treasurer, and PUD.
